About

Ultimate Controller is a free gamepad tester for checking PS5, PS4, Xbox Series X|S and Xbox One controllers right in your browser. No download and no account.

It's an independent project, built to be a cleaner, more useful controller tester. Along with showing every button, it measures stick drift and gives you real deadzone numbers to use in your games.

What it does

  • Live view of every button, trigger and stick
  • A checklist so you know every input has been tested
  • Stick calibration with deadzone and range previews
  • Rumble testing on controllers and browsers that support it, including the trigger motors on Xbox controllers where the browser allows it
  • Automatic detection of PS5, PS4, Xbox Series X|S and Xbox One controllers

Contact

Found a bug, or a controller that doesn't show up right? Open an issue on GitHub and include your controller model and browser.
